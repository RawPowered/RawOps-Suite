For Vendors: Seamless Integration

We eliminate the friction of multi-platform sales. Vendors get fully customizable, domain-ready storefronts. More importantly, our new Shopify and WooCommerce plugins allow existing stores to sync their products and manage Product affiliates separately, making adoption effortless.

Product dramatically lowers setup barriers while maximizing vendor control and brand identity.

For Affiliates: Building Prestige

We are ending the "money grab" culture of typical affiliate programs. Product affiliation is invite-only, offered to customers *after* their first purchase. This creates a genuine, exclusive community of brand advocates, all managed through a single, unified dashboard.

Ecosystem Expansion: Beyond Products

Our ecosystem creates its own economy. We are launching a Hiring Board for platform-specific jobs (sales agents, shop managers) and offering new services like product image optimization. This diversifies value for everyone and reinforces platform quality.

Projected Value Streams

As the platform matures, our value streams will diversify significantly. While product sales remain core, the growth of our SaaS model and new ecosystem services creates a more resilient and profitable business model.

  • Product Sales: Commission from marketplace transactions.
  • SaaS Fees: Recurring revenue from vendor storefronts & tools.
  • Ecosystem Services: Fees from the Hiring Board and optimization services.
