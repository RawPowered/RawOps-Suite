**November 20, 2025 is the latest recorded send in the available snapshot. The snapshot cannot establish the business's final operating date.** Its most recent send falls on the export date, so absence of later records is expected. A later shutdown cannot be dated from this earlier backup.

The export header and application timestamps may use different time conventions. Dates are reported as stored; no timezone conversion or exact sequence across different timestamp sources is asserted. Recipient identities, message content, tokens and database records remain private.

## Reading the portfolio accurately

- Historical screenshots show the interface and recorded values at their capture dates.
- Source and migrations show implementation and changes preserved in the archive.
- Retained email and workflow records support a bounded historical usage account.
- Business inactivity, marketing cessation and the site's offline status are supplied by my firsthand account. Their exact calendar dates remain unconfirmed by the available archive.
- The showcase claims no current campaign activity, attributable sales, ongoing availability or successful restoration.

The operating period, the site's availability period and the date a backup was taken are separate parts of the history. A later dated archive or contemporaneous record would be needed to establish the final activity and shutdown dates more precisely.
