# Evidence capture guide

This guide covers the real, archived screen captures included with the public archive and the redaction standard used for them.

## Included evidence

The user provided a 193-image archive under the original project folder. Eleven genuine captures were selected, copied as fresh PNG files, redacted with solid black blocks where needed, and added to the [evidence gallery](GALLERY.md). The source originals remain outside this repository.

The selected screens show operations, CRM quality, module navigation, training progression, the assistant interface, email composition, an agent business card, legal access, a legal evidence timeline and a historical Lighthouse audit. Captions identify capture dates and evidence limits. The images show archived screens and the values visible at that time; they do not prove current system availability, current data, business outcomes, provider health or current security posture.

The rest of the archive remains private. It contains identifying customer and staff records, legal case material, old product and company branding, and repeated or partial views. The diagrams in docs/diagrams/ are source-derived illustrations, not screenshots.

## Redaction standard

- Work from a copy and retain the original privately.
- Use opaque solid redaction for sensitive regions. Pixelation can leave names or text recoverable.
- Remove browser profile images, account names, email, phone, address, customer and product identity, order IDs, legal case details, balances, credentials and URL parameters.
- Review the full image at native size, including browser chrome and status bars.
- Strip metadata that may contain account, device or location information.
- Caption each image with the feature and a careful evidence label.
- Tie each image to relevant source paths in the capability catalog.
